# Episode 1 Remaster: *Operation: Barefoot Banana* (行動代號：赤腳香蕉) — Full 15-Shot Script & Storyboard

## 1. Episode Overview (集數概覽)

- **Title**: *Operation: Barefoot Banana* (行動代號：赤腳香蕉)
- **Visual Style Lock (100% Unified with Episode 2 Director's Cut)**:
  - **2020s High-Budget Modern Anime Cinema**: Ufotable and Makoto Shinkai cinematic lighting style, ultra-detailed digital painting, delicate thin lineart, volumetric lighting, ray-traced marble/wet-concrete reflections, glossy fabric, and realistic glossy sheer pantyhose textures. Strictly **NO** 1990s flat cel-shading or thick comic ink outlines.
- **Core Character Canon & Continuity Bridges into Episode 2**:
  - **Golden Eagle Crest Smuggling Network**: Confiscated dockside crates bear the **Golden Eagle Crest (金色飛鷹徽章)** alongside encrypted Gala invitations (`Ep1_Shot_01` ➔ `Ep1_Shot_14`), pointing directly to Episode 2's Charity Gala & **Gideon Ward** (`Ep1_Shot_15` ➔ `Ep2_Shot_08` / `Ep2_Shot_20`).
  - **Project Simian & Barnaby's Adoption**: **Maya Tachibana** (`橘 瑪雅`) loses her burgundy high heels naturally mid-jump (`Ep1_Shot_03`), steps onto a banana peel beside a cracked military crate marked **Project Simian**, bonds with an escaped high-IQ recon orangutan wearing an old chip collar (`Ep1_Shot_04`–`08`), and signs Commander Griz's Special Tactical Asset Custody Order in `Ep1_Shot_14`—naming him **Barnaby** with an agent lapel pin.
  - **Oliver Kade's Grapple-Switch Warning**: In `Ep1_Shot_02`, **Oliver Kade** hands **Agent Saffron Sinclair** her **matte-black prototype compact tactical pistol** and explicitly warns her not to flip the micro-wire grapple switch—directly foreshadowing Saffron's catwalk zipline crash (`Ep1_Shot_10`–`11`) and recurring Episode 2 pistol-wire chaos (`Ep2_Shot_03`, `Ep2_Shot_14`, `Ep2_Shot_17`).
  - **Victoria Parker (Raven) Signal Hook**: Also in `Ep1_Shot_02`, **Emily Parker** asks Oliver for any new trace of her missing older sister **Victoria Parker**, and Oliver stiffens evasively—directly setting up Oliver's covert cybernetic arm check in `Ep2_Shot_04` and Aoi's investigation in `Ep2_Shot_05`.
  - **Strict Non-Speaking Rule for Barnaby**: Barnaby is an expressive but **strictly non-speaking** orange orangutan; he keeps his mouth closed during dialogue and appears at the end of H3 prompt descriptions as `a quiet orange ape` to prevent voice-stealing.
