# Episode 2: Preliminary Script Outline & Storyboard Framework (第二集初步劇本大綱與分鏡框架)

## 1. Episode Overview (集數概覽)
- **Working Title**: *Operation Golden Mask / The Gala Gambit* (暫名：金面具行動／晚宴博弈)
- **Core Goal**: Transition from Episode 1's slapstick origin into a full-scale structured mission that weaves together:
  1. **Field Comedy Trio + Barnaby** (Maya, Saffron, Chloe Han, and Barnaby as Maya's loyal slapstick partner-in-crime).
  2. **Specialist Trio** (Evelyn Carter, Naomi Brooks, Isabella Chen) and their dynamic friction with the comedy crew.
  3. **Military Conspiracy & Shadow Arc** (Victoria Parker / Raven's MIA investigation via Aoi Fujimoto, Elise Mori, and Emily Parker; political maneuvering by Minister Helena Voss; and the looming threat of **The Colossus**).
- **New Character Integrations (Episode 2 Debuts)**:
  - **Oliver Kade / Patch (奧利佛・凱德／補丁)**: HQ Gadget & Weapons Quartermaster (late-20s tech genius arguing with Saffron over the Banana Blaster and secretly maintaining Victoria's cybernetic arm).
  - **Finn Calder / Trigger (芬恩・考爾德／觸發器)**: Unlucky Rookie Male Agent (spiky orange-haired competent fighter caught in Maya, Saffron & Barnaby's slapstick crossfire).
  - **Nia Vale (妮雅・維爾)**: 22-year-old fashion student & part-time gala event assistant (acquaintance of Airi Fujimoto) who accidentally witnesses a clandestine syndicate handoff.
  - **Gideon Ward / The Broker (基甸・沃德／經紀人)**: Calculating Syndicate Mastermind overseeing **The Colossus** and connected to Victoria's MIA battle.
  - **Dorian Marlowe / Mirror (多利安・馬洛／鏡像)**: Narcissistic Male Rival Agent deployed by Minister Helena Voss; a suave "James Bond" Special Liaison Operative who competes to outshine Saffron but gets repeatedly dragged into Maya and Saffron's slapstick chaos.

## 2. Four-Act Structural Outline (四幕劇本大綱框架)

### Act I: HQ Briefing, Gadget Chaos & Sisterly Bonds (第一幕：總部簡報、裝備鬧劇與姊妹情深)
- **Location**: `HQ Operations & Command Center` & `Military Intelligence Comms & Decryption Hub`
- **Plot Beat 1 (HQ Briefing)**:
  - **Commander Griz** briefs all 6 field agents on a high-society charity gala hosted at the **Luxury Marble Corridor / Gala Venue**, where a black-market syndicate linked to **The Colossus** plans a secret data exchange.
  - **Specialist Trio vs. Comedy Crew Sparks**:
    - **Evelyn (Vermilion)** mocks **Saffron**'s "elite agent" posturing.
    - **Naomi (Violet)** deadpans while pulling oversized tactical gear out of her small pink handbag.
    - **Isabella (Phoenix)** smilingly opens a secret betting pool on how many minutes it will take before Saffron or Dorian Marlowe triggers the gala alarm.
  - **Maya & Barnaby (Protective Buddy-Cop Duo)**: Maya attends in full uniform (wearing her high heels over sheer pale brown pantyhose), with **Barnaby** sitting loyally beside her as her devoted guardian—trying to salute like a real agent while holding a banana.
- **Plot Beat 2 (Quartermaster Lab / Comms Hub)**:
  - **Oliver Kade (Patch)** hands out upgraded gear in the Quartermaster Lab and gets into a heated argument with Saffron over why she keeps jamming the **Banana Blaster**, while quietly checking diagnostics linked to **Victoria Parker's cybernetic arm**.
  - **Emily Parker**, **Aoi Fujimoto (Lethe)**, and **Elise Mori** coordinate mission logistics. Aoi privately spots an encrypted signature matching the battle where **Victoria Parker (Raven)** went MIA, vowing again to find the truth while protecting Emily from the darkest details.

### Act II: High-Society Gala Infiltration & Rival Interference (第二幕：高級晚宴潛入與政務部特工亂入)
- **Location**: `Luxury Marble Corridor / High-Security Gala Venue` & `Ministry of Political Affairs`
- **Plot Beat 1 (Glamour, Witness & Undercover Entry)**:
  - Supermodel **Airi Fujimoto** graces the gala red carpet, unintentionally providing cover for the Specialist Trio (**Evelyn, Naomi, Isabella**) to infiltrate the VIP lounge.
  - Fashion student & part-time gala event assistant **Nia Vale** (in her red devil-horn headband, leather miniskirt, and sheer light-brown tights) accidentally spots a clandestine syndicate data handoff in the dusk corridor.
  - **Minister Helena Voss** attends as the Continental Union's political power broker, secretly dispatching her narcissistic Special Liaison Operative **Dorian Marlowe (Mirror)** to secure the syndicate's target data before Griz's squad can touch it.
- **Plot Beat 2 (Corridor Laser-Grid & Buddy-Cop Slapstick)**:
  - **Saffron, Maya, Chloe, Barnaby, and rookie Finn Calder (Trigger)** take the back marble corridor route lined with floor-level red laser beams.
  - Saffron attempts a "flawless Johnny English" acrobatic vault to upstage **Dorian Marlowe**, which backfires spectacularly—tangling Dorian's immaculate double-breasted suit.
  - Trying to help **Maya**, **Barnaby** enthusiastically mimics spy hand-signals and hands Maya a peeled banana instead of her laser-decoder, while tossing the banana peel right under Dorian's polished dress shoes—sending Dorian and Saffron crashing straight into unlucky rookie **Finn Calder**, while Barnaby heroically hoists Maya onto his shoulder above the laser grid and **Chloe Han** calmly sips her tea with pinky extended.

### Act III: The Colossus Crashes the Party (第三幕：巨像襲擊與雙線交鋒)
- **Location**: `Luxury Marble Corridor / High-Security Gala Venue (Main Vault & Terrace)`
- **Plot Beat 1 (The Unstoppable Enforcer)**:
  - **The Colossus** (210 cm golden-armored juggernaut in the smiling metal mask) smashes into the vault area to retrieve the syndicate core. Standard gunfire and **Dorian Marlowe's** flashy gadget pen bounce harmlessly off his armor.
- **Plot Beat 2 (Team Combo & VIP Sixth-Ranger Ace)**:
  - **Evelyn, Naomi, and Isabella** execute coordinated crowd evacuation and psychological/tactical distraction.
  - **Chloe Han** steps forward as the VIP Ace, unleashing lethal high-kick Taekwondo combos with ladylike grace to deflect The Colossus's warhammer swing, while **Maya, Barnaby, and Saffron** team up to trigger an environmental chandelier/banana-blaster trap that stalls the juggernaut.
- **Plot Beat 3 (Shadow Intervention — Raven Appears)**:
  - Just as The Colossus prepares a berserk counterattack, dark mechanical/feathered wings slice through the moonlight—**Victoria Parker (Raven)** descends briefly from the shadows (in her red-and-black tactical outfit and 10D light brown pantyhose), clashing cybernetic arm against golden battleaxe before vanishing into the night before Emily or Aoi's comms camera can fully lock onto her.

### Act IV: Aftermath, Debrief & Shadow Teaser (第四幕：任務善後、總部吐糟與暗線伏筆)
- **Location**: `HQ Operations & Command Center` & `Metropolis Rooftop (Night)`
- **Plot Beat 1 (Comic Relief Debrief)**:
  - Back at HQ, **Commander Griz** vents a massive cloud of steam from his respirator mask over the gala repair bill, while **Oliver Kade** fumes over his ruined gadgets.
  - **Isabella** collects her betting winnings; **Maya and Barnaby** share a celebratory high-five and a bunch of bananas while **Dorian Marlowe** despairs over his wrinkled designer suit.
- **Plot Beat 2 (Conspiracy Cliffhanger)**:
  - **Aoi and Elise** isolate a single clear frame of **Victoria's cybernetic arm and 10D pantyhose silhouette** from the gala security feed, confirming beyond doubt that Raven is alive.
  - Elsewhere in a covert syndicate operations room illuminated by amber holo-displays, **Gideon Ward (The Broker)** calmly reviews combat telemetry from **The Colossus**, setting the stage for Episode 3.
