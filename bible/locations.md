# Locations & Recurring Backgrounds (地方與背景設定庫)

## 1. HQ Operations & Command Center (特工總部指揮中心)
- Affiliation: Commander Griz's Special Operations Division (Continental Union).
- Visual & Lighting Anchor: High-tech war room with a giant curved holographic tactical display in the center, sleek dark metallic walls, cool cyan interface glow mixed with overhead tactical spotlights, glass briefing table.
- Key Occupants: Commander Griz, Emily Parker, and the 6 field agents.

## 2. Military Intelligence Comms & Decryption Hub (軍情處情報及解密中心)
- Affiliation: Continental Union Military Intelligence.
- Visual & Lighting Anchor: Multi-monitor workstation arrays, encrypted server racks with subtle purple and green indicator lights, organized desks, large window overlooking a modern metropolis skyline.
- Key Occupants: Aoi Fujimoto (Lethe) and Elise Mori.

## 3. Luxury Marble Corridor / High-Security Gala Venue (高級雲石長廊與潛入會場)
- Visual & Lighting Anchor: Polished reflective marble floors, tall neoclassical columns, cool blue moonlight streaming through tall arched windows contrasting with warm amber wall sconces, thin red laser security beams near floor level.
- Plot Role: Frequent infiltration target and high-society gala venue attended by Airi Fujimoto and Minister Helena Voss.

## 4. Ministry of Political Affairs Executive Office (政務部部長辦公室)
- Affiliation: Continental Union Civilian Government.
- Visual & Lighting Anchor: Grand neoclassical government building exterior with Continental Union eagle emblem (no real-world flags); interior features floor-to-ceiling bookshelves, mahogany desk, warm golden afternoon light.
- Key Occupants: Minister Helena Voss.
