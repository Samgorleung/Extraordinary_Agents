# Character Roster, Running Gags & Relationship Matrix

## 1. Core Field Agents & Comedy Crew (Commander Griz's Squad)

### Agent Maya Tachibana (橘 瑪雅 / 瑪雅・橘)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Adult woman with wavy shoulder-length brown hair, earpiece headset, tailored reddish-brown ribbed sleeveless blouse, dark brown mini skirt with ID badge clipped at waist, sheer pale brown pantyhose, and burgundy/dark-red high heels (worn by default; may occasionally lose a shoe naturally in a slapstick scramble, though not forced into every scene).
- **Persona & Running Gag (Earnest Airhead Who Fails Upwards / 一本正經做傻事卻錯有錯著)**:
  - **100% Serious Doing Silly Things**: Externally, Maya projects the poised posture, sharp gaze, and unwavering confidence of a top-tier elite operative—yet internally she is an endearing, pure-hearted airhead who does completely silly, clueless things with **100% a warm, approachable, radiant and confident smile that makes everyone trust her talent on first impression, while earnestly doing silly things**.
  - **Accidental Genius (錯有錯著立大功)**: Whenever Maya earnestly says something clueless (e.g., making a solemn observation about a ripening banana) or performs a silly action (e.g., crouching down with deadpan focus to pick up a dropped snack or fix her heel), enemies and rival agents like **Dorian Marlowe** wildly over-interpret her moves as brilliant tactical masterstrokes—causing her to accidentally dodge lethal traps, trigger hidden vault switches, and solve the mission while **Finn Calder** and **Saffron** watch in disbelief.
- **Arc & Bond with Barnaby**: Prior to Episode 1, Maya and Barnaby were complete strangers; losing her shoes during Episode 1's chaotic infiltration was their **origin story** that turned them into inseparable best friends. From Episode 2 onward, she and Barnaby operate on the exact same earnest, clueless wavelength as loyal partners-in-crime—doing absurd things together with deadpan gravity that somehow always save the day.

### Agent Saffron Sinclair (薩芙蓉・辛克萊)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Adult woman with a sleek black updo, elegant black halter dress with a high side slit, sheer grey thigh-high garter stockings with thigh holster, long black opera gloves, black ankle-strap high heels. Wields a **sleek matte-black compact tactical pistol** (equipped with suppressed rounds, stun darts, and a retractable micro-wire grappling hook) holstered on her right thigh garter strap.
- **Personality & Running Gag**: Maya's senior partner. Overconfident, dramatic operative who desperately wants to look like an ultra-cool, legendary secret agent and constantly tries to outshine **Evelyn Carter** and **Dorian Marlowe**, but her flashy stunts and obsession with dramatic entrances always backfire into hilarious slapstick disasters (grappling-wire tangles, crashing ziplines, firing the wrong gadget mode mid-pose).

### Agent Chloe Han (韓克洛伊) & White Rabbit
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Adult woman with dark bob hair and a blue-and-white floral hair ornament, intricate blue-and-white floral evening gown with sheer flowing sleeves and high side slit, sheer skin-tone stockings with a jeweled thigh garter strap, silver-white floral ankle-strap high heels. Accompanied everywhere by her white rabbit wearing a blue ribbon bow.
- **Personality & Running Gag**: The squad's **VIP Sixth Ranger / Ultimate Ace**. Maintains imperturbable, ladylike elegance and deadpan calm—keeping her pinky extended while sipping tea as chaos unfolds around her, before effortlessly clearing the room with lethal, graceful **Taekwondo high-kick martial arts** and heavy firepower.

### Barnaby the Orangutan
- **Visual Anchor**: Large, highly expressive orangutan with long arms, often carrying a banana.
- **Persona & Bond**: Originally an unaligned orangutan encountered by chance in Episode 1. After bonding with Maya during their chaotic first encounter, he became her **devoted protective guardian and partner-in-crime**. Fiercely protective whenever Maya is threatened by enemies, yet overly playful during quiet missions—trying to imitate spy hand-signals, handing Maya a banana instead of a weapon magazine, or tossing banana peels under rival agents' feet.
- **STRICT AUDIO RULE**: Barnaby is a non-speaking orangutan. He NEVER speaks human language in any version and keeps his mouth closed during dialogue.

### Agent Evelyn Carter (Codename: Vermilion / 朱紅)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Light brown wavy long hair, white short-sleeve collared top, red mini skirt, black fishnet stockings (黑色網襪), red high heels.
- **Personality & Role**: Confident, mature, slightly haughty specialist excelling in social engineering, high-society infiltration, and intelligence gathering. Loves teasing and looking down on Saffron's "elite agent" posturing.

### Agent Naomi Brooks (Codename: Violet / 紫羅蘭)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Warm bronze skin, voluminous dark curly hair, black turtleneck sweater, beige pleated mini skirt, **sleek small pink crossbody handbag** (containing realistic, palm-sized concealed spy tools such as a metallic lipstick micro-taser, compact-mirror signal decoder, and micro-EMP discs), black ankle boots.
- **Personality & Role**: Independent, decisive, highly vigilant field agent; delivers deadpan snark and deploys compact, palm-sized spy gadgets from her small pink handbag while remaining deeply protective of her teammates.

### Agent Isabella Chen (Codename: Phoenix / 鳳凰)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: East Asian features, long black wavy hair with a large red ribbon bow, ornate red-and-gold phoenix embroidered high-slit gown (or blue-and-white floral dress in Ep1), gold-patterned sheer stockings, red high heels.
- **Personality & Role**: Elegant, composed, meticulous elite operative. Appears gentle and dignified, but is a smiling schemer, master of psychological warfare, and runs a secret HQ betting pool on her teammates' mission mishaps.

### Agent Finn Calder (芬恩・考爾德 / Codename: Trigger 觸發器)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Young adult male with short spiky orange hair, serious amber eyes, athletic build, bright orange utility/training jacket with dark patches, black T-shirt/undershirt, dark gray/black cargo trousers with tactical pouches, sturdy gray lace-up combat boots.
- **Personality & Running Gag**: New male rookie field agent assigned to Commander Griz's squad. Earnest, determined, and tactically capable in combat, but possesses terrible luck—perpetually getting caught in the crossfire of Maya, Barnaby, and Saffron's slapstick chaos.

## 2. HQ Command, Military Intelligence & Civilian Support

### Commander Marcus "Griz" Grisham (馬庫斯・「葛瑞茲」・格里沙姆 / 葛瑞茲指揮官)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Age 45, 190 cm, 102 kg, muscular grizzled build, buzz cut, clean-shaven/light-stubble stern face (NO mask), black tactical combat vest and uniform, tactical gloves, wristwatch.
- **Dual Role & Running Gag**:
  - **Serious Side**: Supreme Tactical Commander of the Special Operations Team (SOT). His callsign **"Griz"** comes from both his surname **Grisham** and his grizzled front-line reputation. Decisive, protective leader ("I can accept mission failure, but I will never easily sacrifice a subordinate") who shrewdly uses Maya and Saffron's sheer unpredictability as a tactical weapon. Coordinates closely with Military Intelligence (Aoi & Elise) and knows partial classified truths about Victoria Parker's disappearance.
  - **Comedy Side**: Perpetually left in deadpan, soul-crushed exasperation—rubbing his temples with a heavy sigh over the chaotic collateral damage left behind by Maya, Saffron, and Barnaby.

### Emily Parker (艾蜜莉・帕克)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Long wavy brown hair, emerald green eyes, red blazer jacket, blue tight mini skirt, red high heels.
- **Personality & Relationships**: Kind, meticulous, highly efficient HQ Administrative Staff (non-combatant). Younger biological sister of **Victoria Parker**. Looked after like a little sister by **Aoi Fujimoto** and **Elise Mori**.

### Aoi Fujimoto / 藤本葵 (Codename: Lethe)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Long purple hair with tactical headset/hairpiece, purple military comms uniform jacket, dark blue mini skirt, purple high heels.
- **Personality & Relationships**: Calm, rational, strict military intelligence analyst and decryptor (non-combatant). Best friend (like sisters) to **Victoria Parker**; refuses to believe Victoria is dead ("Until I find Victoria's body, I will never accept that she is dead") and secretly investigates her final battle. Older sister of fashion model **Airi Fujimoto**. Treats Emily Parker like her own younger sister.

### Elise Mori (愛麗絲・莫里)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Age 25, European/Nordic features, blonde hair in a ponytail, blue eyes, dark green military administrative uniform skirt suit, **10D light brown sheer pantyhose**, red high heels. (Fictional cross-sector liaison officer; NO real-world flags).
- **Personality & Relationships**: Cheerful, proactive, approachable, occasionally slightly clumsy but highly capable logistics and operations coordinator. Aoi Fujimoto's trusted partner ("Aoi finds the answers, and I make sure everyone can reach them!"). Also dotes on Emily like a younger sister and helps Aoi investigate Victoria's disappearance.

### Oliver Kade (奧利佛・凱德 / Codename: Patch 補丁)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Late-twenties adult male, warm brown wavy side-swept hair, square dark-rimmed glasses, white long-sleeve collared shirt with rolled-up sleeves, fitted charcoal waistcoat, dark tailored trousers, brown leather dress shoes.
- **Personality & Relationships**: HQ Gadget & Weapons Quartermaster. Inventive, analytical, and highly precise tech genius who built and maintains Saffron's **custom multi-mode tactical pistol** and engineered/supports **Victoria Parker's cybernetic mechanical arm**. Constantly argues with and gets exasperated by Saffron's reckless equipment abuse (such as firing her grappling wire indoors just to strike a dramatic pose), yet remains deeply committed to keeping every agent alive through his gear.

## 3. Civilian, Political & Shadow Figures

### Victoria Parker (維多利亞・帕克 / Codename: Raven 渡鴉)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Brown hair in a ponytail, red-and-black tactical combat outfit, black mini skirt, **10D light brown ultra-sheer pantyhose (10 Denier Light Brown Pantyhose)** with subtle silk sheen, black leather shoes, and a **cybernetic mechanical arm**.
- **Awakened Flight State**: Temporarily unfolds giant dark mechanical/feathered wings for high-speed aerial combat (retaining her 10D light brown pantyhose and tactical attire); prolonged use strains her body.
- **Lore & Core Drive**: Former elite military officer listed as MIA after a mysterious battle. Operating in the shadows to protect her younger sister **Emily Parker** while hunting down the truth of her past ("She can abandon her rank, honor, and identity, but she will never abandon her sister").

### Airi Fujimoto / 藤本愛莉
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Age 22, 178 cm, model physique, long wavy dark brown hair with cool violet nuances, violet-brown eyes. Wears a light pink/beige polka-dot short-sleeve blouse with a deep V-neckline and model ID lanyard (Lumière Model Agency), black high-slit tight mini skirt, and black pointed-toe patent high heels. Often seen with a ginger tabby cat.
- **Role & Relationships**: Younger sister of **Aoi Fujimoto** (1 year younger). Famous professional fashion model whose high-society and media connections frequently intersect with the agents' undercover missions.
- **CIVILIAN STATUS**: Non-combatant civilian. Do NOT assign to tactical spy missions or vault battles.

### Nia Vale (妮雅・維爾)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Age 22, adult woman with warm brown skin, waist-length wavy dark hair, small red decorative devil-horn headband (fashion accessory, non-supernatural), gold hoop earrings, fitted red ribbed turtleneck, black leather jacket, short black leather miniskirt with a side slit, **sheer light-brown tights/pantyhose**, glossy black ankle-strap high heels, and a small black bag.
- **Role & Relationships**: Fashion student and part-time event assistant at high-society galas in the Central Sector; a fashion-community acquaintance of **Airi Fujimoto**. Witty, nightlife-loving, and sharp-eyed—accidentally witnesses a clandestine syndicate information handoff during the Episode 2 gala, drawing her into the agents' orbit.
- **CIVILIAN STATUS**: Non-combatant civilian. Do NOT assign to tactical spy missions or vault battles.

### Helena Voss (海倫娜・沃斯)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Age 38, 180 cm, sophisticated updo brown hair, gold hoop earrings, tailored dark brown/black long double-breasted coat-dress, **sheer nude pantyhose with small black polka dots (dotted sheer)**, and white pointed high heels.
- **Role & Relationships**: **Political Affairs Minister** (Civilian Leadership / Government Liaison of the Continental Union). Composed, sharp, and diplomatic power broker bridging the civilian government, military intelligence, and special operations.

### Dorian Marlowe (多利安・馬洛 / Codename: Mirror 鏡像)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Age 31, 186 cm, handsome adult male with short tousled jet-black hair with side-swept bangs and sharp dark eyes. Wears an immaculate tailored black double-breasted suit with gold buttons, crisp white dress shirt, black tie with gold tie pin, white pocket square, Ministerial Liaison ID badge on chest, black leather gloves, and polished black dress shoes. Equipped with aviator sunglasses, Continental Union cufflinks, luxury wristwatch, concealed earpiece, multi-function gadget pen, and concealed sidearm.
- **Personality & Running Gag**: Special Liaison Operative / Diplomatic Black-Ops Rival Agent deployed directly by Political Affairs Minister **Helena Voss** to compete with and outshine Commander Griz's Special Operations Team—especially **Agent Saffron**. Highly stylish, narcissistic, charismatic, competitive, and theatrical. Favors charm, infiltration, and social engineering over brute force, yet despite his immaculate gentleman-spy image, he is repeatedly dragged into the absurd slapstick chain reactions caused by **Maya**, **Saffron**, and **Barnaby**, leaving him in offended disbelief as he is forced to clean up the fallout.

### Gideon Ward (基甸・沃德 / Codename: The Broker 經紀人)
- **Visual & Outfit Anchor**: Age 39, 188 cm, rugged adult man with messy swept-back dark brown hair, neat full beard, stern weathered face with intense gray eyes, dark brown waxed-canvas field jacket over an open gray button-up shirt and black T-shirt, brown leather belt, dark brown cargo trousers, brown leather lace-up field boots, and a black field watch; carries an encrypted smartphone and a restricted syndicate dossier.
- **Abilities & Lore**: Patient, calculating **Syndicate Mastermind** who oversees the invulnerable juggernaut **The Colossus**. Rarely raises his voice, controlling operatives and political targets through confidential intelligence and ruthless leverage. Directly tied to the covert operation and battle where **Victoria Parker (Raven)** went MIA.

### The Colossus (巨像)
- **Visual Anchor**: 210 cm, 165 kg towering juggernaut clad in heavy golden armor and an eerie smiling metal mask, wielding a giant battleaxe and warhammer.
- **Abilities & Lore**: Cruel, arrogant high-ranking enforcer of a mysterious armed syndicate under **Gideon Ward (The Broker)**. Possesses superhuman strength, extreme armor defense, and abnormal regeneration (immune to standard gunfire/explosives). Directly connected to the battle where **Victoria Parker** went MIA and holds clues to the origin of her awakened power.
