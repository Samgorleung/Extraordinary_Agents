# World Setting: Fictional Alternate-Modern Metropolis (Continental Union)

- **World & Polities**: A stateless, alternate-modern world governed by fictional polities such as the **Continental Union** (e.g., Central Sector, Eastern Sector). High-tech espionage, military intelligence, fashion, and secret experiments coexist.
- **No Real-World Nations**: Real-world countries (Japan, Germany, China, USA, etc.) and real-world national flags DO NOT exist. Convert any real-world nationality references into pure physical phenotypes and fictional sector affiliations.
- **Dual Tone Balance**:
  1. **Action-Comedy & Running Gags (Field Team)**: Classic spy-parody comedy built around distinct character contrasts: **Maya's "Tsumeko Takano / 無能之鷹" archetype** (projecting an ultra-competent elite aura while doing completely silly things with 100% earnestness—and somehow succeeding by sheer accident alongside her loyal orangutan partner **Barnaby**), **Saffron's** clumsy "female Johnny English" overconfidence, **Dorian Marlowe's** narcissistic rivalry getting ruined by Maya & Saffron's chain reactions, **Finn Calder's** earnest bad luck (acting as the "Hiwada" straight-man to Maya), **Chloe Han's** deadpan tea-drinking elegance paired with lethal Taekwondo high kicks, and **Commander Griz's** soul-crushed exasperation. *(Note: Maya losing her shoes and getting tickled was strictly Episode 1's one-off origin story; from Episode 2 onward she wears her high heels by default to keep action-comedy fresh and avoid AI safety filter false positives.)*
  2. **High-Stakes Conspiracy (Military & Shadow Arc)**: The mysterious MIA disappearance of Victoria Parker (Raven), her bond with sister Emily and best friend Aoi Fujimoto, political intrigue led by Minister Helena Voss, and the invulnerable threat of The Colossus.
