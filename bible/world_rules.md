# World Setting: Fictional Alternate-Modern Metropolis (Continental Union)

- **World & Polities**: A stateless, alternate-modern world governed by fictional polities such as the **Continental Union** (e.g., Central Sector, Eastern Sector). High-tech espionage, military intelligence, fashion, and secret experiments coexist.
- **No Real-World Nations**: Real-world countries (Japan, Germany, China, USA, etc.) and real-world national flags DO NOT exist. Convert any real-world nationality references into pure physical phenotypes and fictional sector affiliations.
- **Dual Tone Balance**:
  1. **Action-Comedy & Running Gags (Field Team)**: Classic spy-parody slapstick, Saffron's clumsy "female Johnny English" disasters, Maya and Barnaby's protective buddy-cop blunders (banana-peel traps, mixed-up gadgets, and accidental chain reactions that ruin rival agent Dorian Marlowe's composure and hit unlucky rookie Finn Calder), Chloe Han's deadpan tea-drinking elegance paired with lethal Taekwondo high kicks, and Commander Griz's soul-crushed exasperation. *(Note: Maya losing her shoes and getting tickled was strictly Episode 1's one-off origin story; from Episode 2 onward she wears her high heels by default to keep action-comedy fresh and avoid AI safety filter false positives.)*
  2. **High-Stakes Conspiracy (Military & Shadow Arc)**: The mysterious MIA disappearance of Victoria Parker (Raven), her bond with sister Emily and best friend Aoi Fujimoto, political intrigue led by Minister Helena Voss, and the invulnerable threat of The Colossus.
