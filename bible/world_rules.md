# World Setting: Fictional Alternate-Modern Metropolis (Continental Union)

- **World & Polities**: A stateless, alternate-modern world governed by fictional polities such as the **Continental Union** (e.g., Central Sector, Eastern Sector). High-tech espionage, military intelligence, fashion, and secret experiments coexist.
- **No Real-World Nations**: Real-world countries (Japan, Germany, China, USA, etc.) and real-world national flags DO NOT exist. Convert any real-world nationality references into pure physical phenotypes and fictional sector affiliations.
- **Dual Tone Balance**:
  1. **Action-Comedy & Running Gags (Field Team)**: Classic spy-parody comedy built around distinct character contrasts: **Maya's earnest-airhead persona** (projecting an ultra-competent elite aura while doing completely silly things with 100% deadpan earnestness—and somehow succeeding by sheer accident alongside her loyal orangutan partner **Barnaby**), **Saffron's** overconfident superspy posturing backfiring into slapstick, **Dorian Marlowe's** narcissistic gentleman-spy rivalry getting ruined by Maya & Saffron's chain reactions, **Finn Calder's** earnest bad luck as the straight-man rookie, **Chloe Han's** deadpan tea-drinking elegance paired with lethal Taekwondo high kicks, and **Commander Griz's** clean-shaven, temple-rubbing exasperation. *(Note: Maya wears her high heels by default from Episode 2 onward so shoeless prompts aren't forced into every scene, while occasional natural shoe-loss mishaps remain welcome when fitting.)*
  2. **High-Stakes Conspiracy (Military & Shadow Arc)**: The mysterious MIA disappearance of Victoria Parker (Raven), her bond with sister Emily and best friend Aoi Fujimoto, political intrigue led by Minister Helena Voss, and the invulnerable threat of The Colossus.
